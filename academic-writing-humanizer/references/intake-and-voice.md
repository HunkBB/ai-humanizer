# Intake, background and voice

Record audience, genre, target, language, length, output type, draft, sources and deadline. Separate outreach email, research-interest note, full proposal and statement of purpose. Follow exact programme requirements over generic templates.

## Register and preference record

Record `genre`, `reader_expertise`, `target_register`, `first_person`, `sentence_complexity`, `terminology`, `paragraph_development`, and `avoid_would_will`. For each preference record its basis: `explicit_user`, `observed_sample`, `provisional_inference`, or `project_default`. Keep preferences editable and separate from authenticated author characteristics.

Use these project defaults unless the current task specifies otherwise:

| Field | Default |
|---|---|
| Formal academic register | Formal, analytical, precise and restrained; reasoning developed in connected prose. |
| Outreach register | Professional and direct; personal address and a concise request are appropriate. Apply the formal profile to an attached proposal. |
| First person | Retain genuine motivation, responsibility and research choices when useful; center methods on the study, data or analysis where that improves the argument. No pronoun quota. |
| Sentence complexity | Retain functional subordinate clauses, passive constructions and nominalizations. Judge intelligibility and logical relations, not length. |
| Terminology | Prefer precise, established disciplinary wording; explain unfamiliar or study-specific terms at the reader's level. |
| Formal-prose modal preference | `avoid_would_will: true`; rephrase newly written body text while preserving conditions, uncertainty and research status. Exact quotations, titles and reference text are exempt. |

The would/will preference is a chosen house style. These verbs are legitimate in academic English; see [Oxford's academic modals guidance](https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/modals-of-probability/) (checked 2026-11-08). Do not infer a general ban on future reference or on may/might/could. Resolve explicit task requirements before applying defaults. Without authentic samples, a target register can be specified while personal voice remains uncalibrated.

## Fact ledger

Record each material claim's ID, wording, source/location, status, permitted wording and uncertainty. Statuses: CV-reported; user-confirmed; source-verified; proposed; disputed; unknown. These statuses are not interchangeable.

Check dates, titles, individual/team contributions, measured/projected gains, coursework/applied expertise, and public/restricted access. Resolve conflicts with current authoritative sources; otherwise flag or omit. A commercial analysis does not establish a causal research result. Do not copy irrelevant contact details, identifiers, medical history or GPA into reusable files.

## Module A

Prefer 2–3 genuine independently written samples in comparable genres. Record authorship information, date, audience, length and known translation/editor/AI involvement. Uploading a file does not authenticate its authorship.

Observe directness, complexity, first-person usage, transitions, qualification and paragraph development. Ground observations in short actual passages. Treat small samples as provisional. Never infer voice from nationality, accent, institution, test scores or a CV. An AI-assisted draft is not an independent personal baseline.

Adapt to the target genre and preference record. Preserve supported preferences without imitating errors. If no suitable sample is available, proceed with the specified register and report `voice_status: uncalibrated`; do not claim an exact personal match. Do not infer English academic style from chat prompts, résumés, interview transcripts or a different author's paper. Label stylistic defaults as editorial choices.

## Actual author decisions

For consequential gaps, ask a concise bundle: What observation prompted the question? Which explanation do you favor? What data can you obtain? Why this design? What would change your view? Offer alternatives where useful. Do not invent a retrospective discovery story or imply that an AI-proposed topic was the author's longstanding interest.
