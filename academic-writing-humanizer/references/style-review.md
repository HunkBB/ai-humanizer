# Separate fidelity from reader experience

Use this for a substantial rewrite. Prefer an independent reviewer who receives the task, evidence and neutral-labelled drafts without the editing rationale or desired verdict. Record reviewers as AI agents when that is what they are. Never describe their judgments as a professor study or a detector test.

## Lane 1: fidelity

Compare against the source and meaning ledger. Check each fact/number, attribution, uncertainty, definition, denominator, time window, weight, exclusion, missingness rule, access claim and proposed/completed distinction. Use `preserved`, `transparent substantive change`, `lost/changed`, or `unresolved`. Identify omissions and additions with passages. Style gains cannot excuse a lost research decision. Report an annex move, rather than counting it as deletion.

## Lane 2: reader experience

For each dimension use `better`, `similar`, `worse`, or `not assessable`, cite text, and explain why:

1. The question and purpose appear promptly.
2. Paragraphs have distinct jobs and connect naturally.
3. Claims are concrete and proportionate; attribution is useful rather than procedural.
4. Syntax reads smoothly without repeated stock openings, false contrasts, ceremonial triples, or forced short sentences.
5. Methods can be followed without rereading stacked definitions and exceptions.
6. The ending gives a real implication, unresolved choice or next step.
7. Voice is consistent with authenticated samples, or explicitly not assessable without them.
8. Formatting, references and residue suit the delivery format.

Do not merge these into an AI percentage, authenticity score or claim of undetectability. Report mixed findings honestly. A stylistic rewrite cannot pass solely on better methodological specificity or fewer keyword matches.

## Disposition and revision

Report a preference with reasons, or no clear preference. If readability is worse, revise the actual passages causing it. If a fidelity error remains, label the draft unfinished. A second pass should target the identified problem; it should not blindly rewrite everything again. Record changed scope/length so the comparison is interpretable.

Retain a concise coverage ledger with `source row`, `scope`, `disposition`, `evidence`, and `action/keep reason`. Full audits require one disposition per source row; parent rows can refer to their children. Allow `not present` and `not applicable` instead of fabricating a finding for every sign.
