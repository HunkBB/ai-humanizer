# Review fidelity, reader experience and register separately

Use this for a substantial rewrite. Prefer an independent reviewer who receives the task, evidence and neutral-labelled drafts without the editing rationale or desired verdict. Record reviewers as AI agents when that is what they are. Never describe their judgments as a professor study or a detector test.

## Lane 1: fidelity

Compare against the source and meaning ledger. Check each fact/number, attribution, uncertainty, definition, denominator, time window, weight, exclusion, missingness rule, access claim and proposed/completed distinction. Use `preserved`, `transparent substantive change`, `lost/changed`, or `unresolved`. Identify omissions and additions with passages. Style gains cannot excuse a lost research decision. Report an annex move, rather than counting it as deletion.

## Lane 2: reader experience

For each dimension use `better`, `similar`, `worse`, or `not assessable`, cite text, and explain why:

1. The question and purpose appear promptly.
2. Paragraphs have distinct jobs and connect naturally.
3. Claims are concrete and proportionate; attribution is useful rather than procedural.
4. Syntax reads smoothly without repeated stock openings, false contrasts, ceremonial triples, or forced short sentences.
5. The target reader can follow the methods without unnecessary lay explanation or stacked undefined terms.
6. The ending gives a real implication, unresolved choice or next step.
7. Voice is consistent with authenticated samples, or explicitly not assessable without them.
8. Formatting, references and residue suit the delivery format.

## Lane 3: register and stated preferences

Use `meets brief`, `needs revision`, or `not assessable`, with passages and reasons:

1. The tone fits the document: formal analytical prose for academic body text; professional directness for outreach email. An attachment follows its own genre.
2. Paragraphs develop the necessary evidence, interpretation and qualification without an imposed template or conversational walkthrough.
3. Vocabulary is precise and idiomatic for the discipline; no ornamental jargon, vague simplification or needless synonym variation.
4. Sentence complexity serves logical relations. No blanket short-sentence rule, passive-voice conversion, nominalization ban or sentence-length quota.
5. First person has an identifiable function; repeated personal action narration is not the default, and valid author choices remain visible.
6. The recorded would/will preference is satisfied in newly authored formal body text. Inspect contractions and classify protected quotes/titles, proper names and nonmodal uses; do not count before/examples or editorial notes as output violations. Any rewrite preserves proposed/completed status, conditions, prediction strength and counterfactual meaning.
7. Full forms and professional phrasing replace casual fillers or asides where appropriate. The result remains intelligible and does not gain formality through noun piles or stock “The proposed study…” openings.
8. An effective source passage is retained or lightly edited; every material rewrite has a reason. Match a genuine personal sample only when available, otherwise evaluate the target register and leave personal voice unassessed.

Do not merge the lanes into an AI percentage, authenticity score or claim of undetectability. Report mixed findings honestly. A stylistic rewrite cannot pass solely on better methodological specificity, fewer keyword matches or a formal tone that impairs comprehension.

## Disposition and revision

Report a preference with reasons, or no clear preference. If readability or register fails the brief, revise the actual passages causing it. If a fidelity error remains, label the draft unfinished. A second pass should target the identified problem; it should not blindly rewrite everything again. Record changed scope/length so the comparison is interpretable.

Retain a concise coverage ledger with `source row`, `scope`, `disposition`, `evidence`, and `action/keep reason`. Full audits require one disposition per source row; parent rows can refer to their children. Allow `not present` and `not applicable` instead of fabricating a finding for every sign.
